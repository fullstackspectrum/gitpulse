# GitPulse brand assets

GitPulse follows the [Full-Stack-Spectrum](https://fullstackspectrum.com) design system.

- `gitpulse-icon.svg`: the mark on a 64×64 `#0a0d12` tile (radius 14), for avatars and app icons. The dashboard inlines it as its tab icon (`ICON` in `src/gitpulse/report.py`); keep the two in sync.
- `gitpulse-mark.svg`: the mark alone, for dark grounds only (its layers are filled `#0a0d12`).
- `readme-banner.png`: 2560×640, shown at 1280×320 at the top of the README.
- `github-social-preview.png`: 1280×640, uploaded under the repository's Settings → Social preview.

The mark is Layered Core with the red core replaced by a pulse line: blue and yellow layers behind a `#eef1f6` outline, cool at the back, hot at the front.
